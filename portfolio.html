Lupfe

Analysis Engineer
Budapest, Hungary · Feb 2017 – May 2019

Conducted analysis studies of an experimental internal combustion engine, developed to be used in industrial electric quadcopter drones as a generator set.

Key responsibilities

  • Designed cooling strategies for the engine head, especially around the combustion chamber (Ansys Fluent).
  • Built steady and transient flow simulations of the intake and exhaust ports (Ansys Fluent).
  • Fuel injection and spray modeling (Ansys Fluent).
  • Airframe stress analysis for a quadcopter drone.
  • Nonlinear static and dynamic stress analysis on composite and metal parts of the airframe, the powertrain and gearboxes.
  • Optimization of engine parts based on structural and thermal stress.
  • Detail design of thermal systems based on 1D, 2D and 3D analysis.
  • Contact stress analysis: gears and sliding surfaces (rockers).
  • Analysis of rotating parts (main shaft, crankshaft) and bearing load distribution.
  • Dynamic structural analysis of the main shaft–rocker–spring system.
  • Meshing, pre- and post-processing, and presenting results to design and management.
  • Documented modeling methods for future engineers.
  • Built and maintained the IT system: ~15 Windows and Linux computers, engineering software (Creo Parametric, ANSYS) and revision control (TortoiseSVN).

Key achievement

  • Completed the engine port design based on our analysis, clearing the engine head for manufacturing and cold-flow testing in 2020.
