Lupfe | Analysis Enginer 2 years 3 months

Analysis Engineer, Feb 2017 – May 2019

Lupfe Lp., Budapest, Hungary

Conducted analysis studies of an experimental internal combustion engine, developed to be used in industrial electric quadcopter drones as a generator set. Mainly worked with Ansys Workbench.

Key responsibilities

  • Designing cooling strategies for the engine head, especially around the combustion chamber (Ansys Fluent)
  • Built steady and transient flow simulations of the intake and exhaust ports (Ansys Fluent)
  • Fuel injection and spray modeling (Ansys Fluent)
  • Airframe stress analysis for a quadcopter drone
  • Nonlinear static and dynamic stress analysis on composite and metal parts of the airframe, the powertrain and gearboxes.
  • Nonlinear static and dynamic stress analysis on composite and metal parts of the airframe, the powertrain and gearboxes.
  • Optimization of various engine parts based on structural and thermal stress
  • Detail design of thermal systems based on 1D-2D and 3D analysis
  • Prepared process documentation of modeling methods for use by future employees
  • Communication with team members and external suppliers to understand requirements and constraints
  • Contact stress analysis, contact of gears, sliding surfaces (rockers)
  • Analysis of rotating parts (main shaft, crank shaft)
  • Bearings load distribution analysis
  • Dynamic structural analysis. Modeling of the main shaft-rocker-spring system.
  • Preparing meshes for static and dynamic cases
  • Pre- and post-processing of simulations
  • Presentation of analysis results to design and management
  • Built and maintained our IT system, a network of ~15 computers (Windows, Linux), engineering software (Creo Parametric, ANSYS), and a revision control system (TortoiseSVN).

Key achievement

  • Due to the completion of the engine port design based on our analysis, the engine head will be manufactured in 2020 with cold-flow testing beginning shortly thereafter.
