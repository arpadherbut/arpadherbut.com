About

Hardware that has to fit, fly and be built.

I design aircraft hardware in the San Francisco Bay Area. Since 2021 I've mainly designed Environmental Control System hardware, taking it from concept through integration, checkout and flight test across multiple aircraft platforms.

Before moving to the U.S. from Hungary in 2019, I worked on internal combustion engine development, part design and manufacturing, and earned my Master's in mechanical engineering, specializing in fluid mechanics.

You can find examples of my work in my portfolio, and my resume is here (PDF).
